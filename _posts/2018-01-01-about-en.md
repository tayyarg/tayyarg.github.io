# About

Hi, I'm **Tayyar Güzel**, an engineer working on wireless communication systems, digital signal processing, machine learning and artificial intelligence.

I started this blog to share what I learn, the problems I run into and how I solve them, in an organized way. I focus especially on these topics:

- **Machine Learning and Bayesian Approaches** — practical applications of methods such as Gaussian process regression, Bayesian inference and MCMC
- **Signal Processing and Wireless Systems** — Kalman filtering, autonomous systems, RADAR/sonar signal processing
- **Artificial Intelligence and Deep Learning** — models adapted to real-world problems (especially with limited data and computing resources)

The posts are usually notes with a strong theoretical foundation but with practical examples. I hope you find something useful here.
