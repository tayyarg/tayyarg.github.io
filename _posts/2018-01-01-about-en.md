# About

This blog explains probabilistic machine learning and signal processing the way I wish they had been explained to me: intuition first, then the math, then code you can run.

Each post is a conversation between **Emre**, who asks the questions a curious engineer would ask, and **Kaan**, who answers them step by step. Every post is available in both **English and Turkish**.

### Where to start

- **Bayesian machine learning:** [Bayesian inference and linear regression](/bayesian-inference-and-linear-regression/) → [Gaussian process regression](/gaussian-process-regression-for-machine-learning/) → [Markov chain Monte Carlo](/markov-chain-monte-carlo-algorithm/)
- **Estimation and signal processing:** [Is DSP dead?](/digital-signal-processing-1-is-dsp-dead/) → [Kalman filter](/kalman-filtering/) → [Adaptive filters and active noise cancellation](/digital-signal-processing-2-adaptive-filters-and-active-noise-cancellation/) → [Compressive sensing and OMP](/digital-signal-processing-3-compressive-sensing-and-orthogonal-matching-pursuit/)
- **A map of the field:** [Essential machine learning algorithms](/essential-machine-learning-algorithms-cheat-sheet/)

### Projects

Longer, hands-on write-ups, each with a complete simulation you can run ([all projects](/projects/)):

- [Game-theoretic TDMA scheduling for long-range WiFi](/project-game-theoretic-tdma-scheduling-for-long-range-wifi/): game theory (Nash equilibrium, Kelly's mechanism, Nash bargaining) applied to sharing airtime in a wireless network
- [Acoustic gunshot detection and localization](/project-acoustic-gunshot-detection-and-localization/): a microphone array, MFCC-based classification and GCC-PHAT direction finding
- [Adaptive compression for emitter location](/project-adaptive-compression-for-emitter-location/): my MSc thesis on TDOA emitter location and bit allocation that preserves location accuracy

### Coming next

- **Wireless communications:** wireless channel models and fading, OFDM, channel estimation and equalization, MIMO, and error-correcting codes such as LDPC

### About me

I'm **Tayyar Güzel**, co-founder of **Vega Networks**, an engineering services company, and SVP of Software Engineering at **SiFly Aviation**, where we build aerial robotics products, namely drones. Our work spans signal processing, estimation, machine learning and wireless communications, and more. I hold an MSc and a PhD in Electrical Engineering, and over the years, my work has also covered power-line communications, energy markets and touchscreen sensing, as the publications and patents below show.
